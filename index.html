Last updated: 1/15/26

Living in Boston, a car can be a convenience—or a major expense. This page helps you compare the true cost of life with a car versus without one, based on how you actually get around. You can explore example lifestyles—such as students, families, or frequent commuters—or use the interactive calculator to estimate costs based on your address and transportation habits.
