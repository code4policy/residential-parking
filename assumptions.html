Assumptions & Methodology

How we calculate costs for the Boston Transportation Cost Calculator

This calculator is designed to estimate the annual cost of owning and using a personal vehicle in Boston, and to compare it with alternative travel options. The numbers below are averages, not guarantees. Actual costs vary by vehicle, neighborhood, driving habits, and market conditions. We only use variable costs (dependent on usage) of owning a car and do not consider the upfront cost of buying a car.

Cost Calculation Formulas

With a Car

Total Annual Cost = Annual Fuel/Electricity Costs + Fixed Annual Costs

Annual Fuel/Electricity costs for non-car owners could be calculated at the AFDC website. For annual mileage, we assume 8.1 miles/commute, 7 miles/non-commute, 120 miles/day trip, and 295 miles/multi-day trip.

Fixed costs include: parking (off-street: $250/month, on-street: free), insurance ($1,200/year), registration & inspection ($100/year), plus loan/lease costs.

Without a Car

Total Annual Cost = (Public Transit Cost × Trips) + (Rideshare/Rental Cost × Trips)

Assumptions are shown below.
MBTA cost: Commute and 75% of non-commute trips use MBTA at $2.4/per ride. If the total cost exceeds $90, the person buys a Charlie card at $90.
Regional bus cost: Average day-trip bus costs $20.
Rideshare cost: 25% of non-commute trips via rideshare @ $15/trip.
Rental car cost: $40/day, assume 2 days for multi-day trips.

Limitations & Disclaimers

  • Averages, not guarantees: All numbers represent Boston-area averages. Your actual costs will vary based on vehicle type, insurance history, neighborhood, and personal driving habits.
  • Public transit availability: This calculator assumes reasonable access to MBTA and regional bus services. Availability varies by neighborhood.
  • Parking assumptions: We assume off-street parking at $250/month or free resident permit parking. Actual costs vary significantly by neighborhood.
  • Household-level estimates: The defaults are for typical households. Your actual patterns may differ.

Questions or feedback? Contact our team to suggest improvements to these assumptions.
